When you save a page

Fill in the description
Uses the text you selected on the page, or the page's own description.
Pick a thumbnail
Uses the page's preview image, or a screenshot when it has none. You can always flip through the choices, take a screenshot or upload your own.
Remember the last folder
Start in the folder you saved to last time, instead of Uncategorized.
Close after saving
Close the popup a moment after a crumb is saved.

The page title and favicon are always filled in.

Shortcuts

  • Alt + Shift + C opens the save popup.
  • Right-click a page, a link or an image and choose Save … to Crumbs. With an image, that image becomes the thumbnail.
  • Select some text before saving and it becomes the description.
  • Paste (Ctrl + V) or drop an image on the thumbnail to use your own.
  • Pages you already saved are spotted, and saving updates them instead of making a copy.
